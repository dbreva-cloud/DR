What is Prime Cut?

A quick orientation before you build

Prime Cut is a metabolic-priming companion for athletes who train BJJ, lift, walk, or mix it all. It reads your steps from HealthKit, builds a personalised meal schedule around your workouts, and uses its Auto Mode Engine to adapt carb targets daily based on activity load and recovery score.

💍
Step Ring
Live HealthKit ring animates as you move. Auto-checks your daily goal.
📅
Workout Planner
Pick BJJ / Strength / Rest. Meals auto-generate with pre & post timing.
⚡
Auto Mode
Adaptive carb engine reads steps + recovery and adjusts macros automatically.
📈
Progress Charts
Weekly adherence line, step bar chart, workout consistency ring.
🛍️
Grocery List
One-tap export to Notes, sized for your training week.
⌚
Watch + Widget
Glanceable step ring, streak, checklist — on your wrist and home screen.

Prerequisites

What you need before opening Xcode
Required
  • ✓
    Xcode 15+ — download from the Mac App Store. Older versions lack Swift Charts and watchOS 10 APIs.
  • ✓
    Apple Developer Account — even a free account works for simulator testing. Paid ($99/yr) is required for real-device HealthKit + App Groups.
  • ✓
    XcodeGen — generates PrimeCut.xcodeproj from project.yml.
  • ✓
    iOS 16+ device or simulator — Swift Charts requires iOS 16. HealthKit steps only work on device.
Install XcodeGen via Homebrew
$ brew install xcodegen

No Homebrew? Install it first: brew.sh

Tip: You do not need to install CocoaPods or Swift Package Manager — Prime Cut has zero third-party dependencies.

Generate & Open the Project

From repo to running in under 2 minutes
In Terminal
$ cd PrimeCut $ xcodegen generate # → PrimeCut.xcodeproj generated $ open PrimeCut.xcodeproj
Configure Signing (one-time)
  • 1
    In Xcode, select the PrimeCut target → Signing & Capabilities.
  • 2
    Set Team to your Apple Developer account.
  • 3
    Change the Bundle ID from com.primecut.app to something unique, e.g. com.yourname.primecut.
  • 4
    Repeat for the PrimeCutWidget and PrimeCutWatchApp targets (keep the same prefix).
  • 5
    Update the App Group ID in PersistenceManager.swift and SharedDataManager.swift to match.
Note: The App Group identifier (group.com.primecut.app) must match across all three targets and both Swift files, or the widget and Watch app won't read live data.
HealthKit Capability
  • ✓
    Already present in project.yml — Xcode will show it under Signing & Capabilities → HealthKit.
  • ✓
    Info.plist keys NSHealthShareUsageDescription and NSHealthUpdateUsageDescription are pre-filled.
Build & Run
# Select "PrimeCut" scheme + your device/simulator # Press ⌘R or click the Play button
Simulator: HealthKit step data won't stream live, but the app shows sample data and all UI works. Test on device for real steps.

Planning Your First Workout

Meals generate automatically around your session
Adding a workout
  • 1
    Tap the Planner tab (calendar icon at the bottom).
  • 2
    Tap + Add Workout at the top of the timeline.
  • 3
    Choose BJJ, Strength, or Rest — each has a different carb multiplier.
  • 4
    Set the start time and duration using the wheel pickers or quick-chips (45 min / 60 min / 90 min).
  • 5
    Tap Save. The timeline immediately shows pre-workout and post-workout meal blocks.
Auto-generated meal times
Pre-workout: 75 min before start
Post-workout: 30 min after end

Breakfast (8 AM), Lunch (1 PM), and Dinner (7 PM) are always present. Workout meals slot in between, replacing any meals that would overlap.

Tapping a meal block
  • ✓
    Opens the Meal Detail Sheet — macros, food suggestions, and a Mark as consumed button.
  • ✓
    Consumed meals update the day's adherence score and checklist.
Logging a completed workout
  • 1
    Tap the clock icon in the Planner toolbar to open Workout History.
  • 2
    Tap + Log Workout.
  • 3
    Set type, date, duration, completion, and RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion, 1–10).
  • 4
    Tap Save — the log auto-checks the "workout logged" checklist item.

Auto Mode Engine

How Prime Cut adapts your carbs daily
How it works

Auto Mode runs silently every time your step count updates or you log a recovery score. It produces one of three signals: Increase, Maintain, or Reduce carbs — then recomputes every meal's macros.

// Priority order (highest wins) if recovery < 40 → Reduce // poor sleep/stress override if steps > goal × 1.3 → Increase if workout == .bjj → Increase if workout == .strength && recovery > 70 → Maintain if workout == .rest && steps < goal × 0.5 → Reduce
Carb multipliers
Increase ×1.15
Maintain ×1.0
Reduce ×0.85
Enabling / Disabling
  • →
    Go to Settings → Auto Mode and toggle the switch.
  • →
    When off, meals use base macros and the dashboard banner is hidden.

Apple Watch Companion

Glanceable ring + checklist on your wrist
Building to Watch
  • 1
    In Xcode, select the PrimeCutWatchApp scheme.
  • 2
    Choose your paired Apple Watch as the destination (or the watchOS simulator).
  • 3
    Press ⌘R. Xcode will install the Watch app through the paired iPhone.
Watch app screens
  • 💍
    Dashboard — animated step ring, step count, streak row, mini checklist bar. Tap anywhere to sync latest data from your phone's App Group.
  • ☑️
    Checklist — scrollable list with check/uncheck state (read-only; modify via the iPhone app).
Data flow: iPhone app → App Group UserDefaults → Watch reads on open or tap. No WatchConnectivity required — both devices read the same shared container.

iCloud Sync

Plans and progress across all your devices
Setup
  • 1
    Ensure iCloud Drive is enabled in iOS Settings → Apple ID → iCloud.
  • 2
    In Xcode, verify the iCloud capability is on for the PrimeCut target with CloudKit checked and container iCloud.com.primecut.app selected.
  • 3
    In the app, go to Settings → iCloud Sync and tap Sync Now to push your week plans and progress.
Requires paid developer account for CloudKit production. Simulator uses the development environment automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common issues and answers
The step ring shows 0 and never updates. ▼
HealthKit permission was likely denied. Go to iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Prime Cut and enable Step Count read access. Then relaunch the app.
The widget shows dashes (—) for all values. ▼
The App Group ID doesn't match between the main app and the widget target. Check that group.com.primecut.app (or your custom ID) is identical in SharedDataManager.swift, PersistenceManager.swift, and the Signing & Capabilities for both targets. Also confirm the App Group is registered in your developer account at developer.apple.com.
Xcode says "No account for team" after running xcodegen. ▼
Sign in to Xcode with your Apple ID: Xcode → Settings → Accounts → + → Apple ID. Then return to Signing & Capabilities for each target and select your team.
Auto Mode keeps reducing my carbs even on training days. ▼
Check your recovery score — a score below 40 triggers the reduce override regardless of workout type. Rate your sleep and stress in the Dashboard recovery card to get an accurate score. Improving your recovery score above 50 will allow Auto Mode to recommend Maintain or Increase.
My streak reset even though I completed everything. ▼
The streak requires all checklist items to be checked on consecutive calendar days. If you completed the checklist but the app wasn't open to record the completion before midnight, the streak may have broken. You can manually reset and resume from Settings → Reset Streak. Future version will use background app refresh to capture late completions.
How do I reset everything for a fresh start? ▼
Go to Settings → Data → Reset All Data. This clears all week plans, progress, workout history, and recovery logs from the device. It does not delete iCloud data — use the iCloud section to wipe cloud records separately. Onboarding will relaunch on next open.
